Hikflow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Hikflow is a private task board used internally by a small team. It is not a public service and is not open to general sign-up. This policy explains what the application stores and how it is used.

Who can use Hikflow

Access is limited to people whose email address has been explicitly added to an allow list by the team. Signing in with a Google account that is not on that list grants no access and no data is created for that account.

What we collect

  • Account information from Google. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, your name, and your Google account identifier. We request no other Google permissions — we cannot read your email, files, contacts or calendar.
  • Content you enter. Projects, applications, tasks, assignees, due dates, links and comments that you or your teammates create in the app.
  • Activity records. The app automatically records who added, edited, moved, completed, deleted or commented on an item, and when, so the team can see the history of a project.

We do not collect payment details, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and the app does not accept image, video or other file uploads.

How it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and is protected by row-level security rules, so only signed-in members on the allow list can read or write it. The application itself is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and holds no data of its own.

How it is used

The information is used only to operate the task board — to show tasks, identify who is responsible for them, and display the activity history to other members of the team. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data is visible to the members of the team who use this board. Our infrastructure providers — Supabase (database and authentication) and GitHub (site hosting) — process data solely on our behalf in order to run the service.

Retention and deletion

Content stays until a team member deletes it. Deleting a project also deletes the tasks and comments inside it. To have your account and the personal information associated with it removed, ask a team administrator to remove your address from the allow list and delete your profile; access ends immediately when your address is removed.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Authentication is handled by Google — Hikflow never sees or stores your Google password. Permission checks are enforced in the database itself, not only in the browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or about the data held about you, please contact the team administrator who invited you to Hikflow.
